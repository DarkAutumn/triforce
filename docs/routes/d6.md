# The magic sword and Dungeon 6 (rest-of-game research, Nov 15-16)

The stretch from the Dungeon 7 triforce (overworld 0x42) through the graveyard to the magic sword (0x21's gravestone)
and level 6's entrance (0x22), then level 6 along the draft path to Gohma and the triforce. It was mined from the
disassembly, the level documents and the project's code before any of it is built. `docs/routes/rest-of-game.md` is
the synthesis over this document and its five siblings (`d7.md`, `d8.md`, `d9.md`, `late-monsters.md`,
`late-mechanics.md`).

**Tags.** **(trace)** observed in the running game per the cited document; **(code)** read from the disassembly
(label cited as `asm:Name`); **(data)** ROM tables (`zelda-asm/data/...`); **(draft)** `docs/routes/first-quest.md`
or `triforce/draft_routes.yaml`; **(yaml)** `triforce/game.yaml`; **(env)** the Rust env/observation code;
**(inferred)** reasoning, not walked. Positions are Link's `ObjX`,`ObjY` in hex. Dungeon floor row r = (Y - `$5D`)/16,
floor column c = (X - `$20`)/16; overworld square row = (Y + `$0B` - `$40`)/16, column = X/16. "Hearts" are before
rings (Link has no ring on this stretch).

**Sources read in full or in the sections that matter:** `zelda-asm/levels/level-6.md` (all of it),

### 1.3 The graveyard (0x40, 0x41, 0x31, 0x21): Ghinis and gravestones

- Each room makes **one Ghini** (`$21`): HP `$90` = 5 white / 3 magic hits; touch 1 heart; wanders at 0.5 px/frame
  homing on the chase target; spawn cloud first step (slot+1)×16 frames. Drop group 1 (59%). (code `# Ghini`)
- **Touching a gravestone** releases a **flying Ghini** (`$22`): when Link, on a grid point with a direction held, is
  blocked by a gravestone tile (`$BC`-`$BF`), a type `$22` appears on that square in the highest free slot, fades in
  `$3F` frames (its touch already hurts on the drawn frames of the fade-in), then flies like a Peahat (up to 0.625
  px/frame, ignores tiles, chases 96/256 of decisions). No limit but free slots; the same gravestone gives another
  once the last has left the square. (code `asm:Spawn_CheckTouchedArmosOrGravestone`, `asm:Armos_InitOrFlyingGhiniFadeIn`)
- A flying Ghini **cannot be hurt, stunned or pushed**; it dies only when the room's Ghini dies (all at once). A
  flying Ghini released **after** the Ghini is dead can never be killed (lives until Link leaves). Touch 1 heart. (code
  `asm:Ghini_Update`, `asm:Ghini_UpdateFlying`)
- Rule for the model: never press into a gravestone square; walk the columns and rows between them (columns 0-2,
  4-5, 7-8, 10-11, 13; rows r2, r4, r6, r8 in all four rooms). (data, inferred)

### 1.4 The one stone of 0x21 that moves, and how it is pushed

- The movable stone is the room's **tile object** (type `$65`, slot `$0B`), at X `$90`, Y `$90` (square r5 c9; the
  draft's "tile (18, 10)"), a Q1-only secret (attr F bits 7-6 = 1). The other 13 gravestones are scenery. (data
  `levelblock-ow` row `$21`: `$65 gravestone at $90,$90`, qs Q1; code `asm:Secret_IsForOtherQuest`)
- Push rule (code `asm:Rock_Update`): no item needed (the rock needs the bracelet, the gravestone not); Link's X
  **exactly** `$90`; |Link Y + 3 - `$90`| < `$11`; the vertical input exactly toward it (Up from below, Down from above);
  **no push timer** (it starts on the first such frame). It slides `$10` px in 32 frames, only up or down; while it
  slides Link cannot move within `$10` px of it (`asm:Link_CheckSlidingTileObjBlocking`).
  - From above: Link at (`$90`,`$7D`) on the open row r4, hold Down. From below: (`$90`,`$9D`) on r6, hold Up.
    Either works by the code; the draft pushes it down from above (draft); yaml says "up or down" (yaml, trace
    Nov 13). r4 is reached from the S entry by going up column c2 then E along r4. (inferred from layout)
- After the slide: stairs appear at the room's shortcut position 2 = (`$90`,`$90`), where it stood (not where it
  stopped); room flag `$20` set, so the stairs are back on every later layout. (code `asm:Rock_Update`,
  `asm:Secret_RestoreStairsOW`; data stairs `2:$90,$90`)
- **Flying Ghini at the stairs (inferred, walk it):** the pushed stone's square is drawn with gravestone tile `$BC`
  (`asm:RoomLayout_TileObjSquareTilesOW`), so the frame Link is blocked by it pushing, the gravestone touch runs too
  and a flying Ghini fades in **on the stairs square** (Link facing down: Y = Link Y + `$13` = `$90`). Stepping onto the
  stairs at (`$90`,`$8D`) then touches it: 1 heart and a `$20`-px knockback. overworld.md: "Not walked then: the 0x21
  gravestone (magic sword; Ghinis kept knocking Link off it)" — consistent with this. The env must expect it: either
  take the hit and use the 48 invincibility frames to step on, or wait the fade-in (63 frames) and for it to fly off.
  If the room's Ghini was killed first, this flying Ghini is unkillable. (code; inferred mechanism)
- Entering: stairs need Link on the stairs tile at Y low nibble `$D` and X a multiple of `$10`: (`$90`,`$8D`). (code
  `asm:Warp_CheckUnderLink`)

### 1.5 The cave and the 12-container test

- Cave `$13`, person `$6D`, text "MASTER USING IT AND YOU CAN HAVE THIS."; wares `- | magic sword | -` with flags
  pick-up, show-items, heart-requirement, no pay. (data `levelblock-ow` caves table)
- Taking: Link's X **exactly `$78`** (the middle ware; `asm:Cave_WareXs` = `$58`,`$78`,`$98`) and |Y - `$98`| < 6
  (Y `$95`/`$9D`). Link enters a cave at X `$70`, so he must sidestep one cell; walking straight up from the entrance
  takes nothing (code; d2first's 0x67 note in overworld.md is the same rule, trace).
- The test: `HeartValues` ≥ `$B0` for any sword person but the white one (`$6C`: ≥ `$40`). The high nibble is
  containers - 1, so this is **exactly "12 containers", whatever the current hearts** (the low nibble cannot change
  the result). (code `asm:Cave_State2TalkOrShopOrDoorCharge@TryToTake`)
- Below 12: **nothing happens**, the person stays, Link can leave and come back later: not irreversible. (code)
- Taken: room flag `$10` set (the person is gone next visit), `InvSword` = 3 (a grade never goes down), Link is halted
  holding it up `$7F` frames; the person disappears `$40` frames later. Text typing halts Link before that (0x67's
  cave: ~170 frames, overworld.md). (code `asm:Item_TakeClass2Grade`, `asm:Item_Take`; trace for 0x67's text)
- At this point Link has exactly 12 containers (D7's container is the 12th) (draft). If a future route change made
  D7's heart optional, the cave would refuse silently: put a container check in the route (inferred).
- Coming out: Link appears at the room's exit spot, which the data gives as (`$50`,`$6D`) for 0x21 (`A & $F0`,
  `(F & 7)*16 + $4D`; data). Not confirmed for a stairs cave by any trace here: walk it (§12). From there column c5
  (X `$50`) is clear down to the S edge, into 0x31 at X `$50`. (data, inferred)

### 1.6 0x32 → 0x22 and the entrance rule of 0x22

- 0x22's only gap from the S is the steps at X `$70` (c7, rows r9-r10). From there straight up X `$70` to the mouth

### 4.1 Shared

### 4.2 Red Wizzrobe: appearance, timing, when it can be hit and when it shoots

- **Cycle** (208 frames, `ObjState` counting down from `$FF`): 1 frame choosing a spot (`$FF`), 63 fading in
  (`$FE`-`$C0`), 64 solid (`$BF`-`$80`), 16 fading out (`$7F` jumps to `$4F`, then to `$40`), 64 hidden (`$3F`-`$00`).
  (code `asm:Wizzrobe_Red_Update`, `asm:Wizzrobe_Red_State1FadeOut`)
- **Where:** one random byte: bits 0-1 pick the side (above, below, left, right of Link) and set the facing toward
  Link; bits 0-3 pick the distance 2, 3, 4 or 5 squares (`$20`/`$30`/`$40`/`$50` px). X = (Link X + offset) AND `$F0`;
  Y = ((Link Y + 3 + offset) AND `$F0`) − 3. The spot is rejected if Y is outside `$5D`-`$C3` or its tile is not walkable
  (X is not range-checked, only the tile); a rejected spot is re-drawn **every frame** (each retry delays that Red's
  cycle one frame and re-reads Link's position). (code `asm:Wizzrobe_Red_State3Appear`, tables
  `asm:Wizzrobe_Red_AppearOffsetsX/Y`, `asm:Wizzrobe_Red_AppearDirs`)
- **When it shoots:** once per cycle at state `$B0`, the 16th solid frame = **79 frames after the spot is chosen**,
  straight along the line it faces, i.e. the line to where Link **was** when it appeared. (code
  `asm:Wizzrobe_Red_State2Solid`)
- **When it can be hit:** every frame of the 64 solid frames; on alternate frames of the 63 fade-in and 16 fade-out
  frames (also when it can hurt Link); never while hidden. (code `asm:Wizzrobe_DrawAndCheckCollisionsIntermittently`)
- **All Reds of a room start the same frame**, so they appear together and shoot together every 208 frames (retries
  shift one by a frame or so). Two Red shots fired the same frame from 32-80 px away reach Link within 16 frames of
  each other: with the 48-frame invincibility, **one volley costs at most one shot = 4 hearts** if Link stands still.
  (code; arithmetic inferred)
- **Line geometry (code; the arithmetic is reasoning, not checked, walk it §12):** shots have attribute `$40` (half
  width): middle = (X + 4, Y + 8); Link's middle (X + 8, Y + 8); a touch needs both distances < 9
  (`asm:Link_CheckMonsterTouch_NoReset`, `asm:Obj_GetMiddle`, attribute `$E1` for `$58`/`$59` in
  `asm:Obj_TypeAttributes`).
  - A **vertical** Red shot (Red above or below) hits only if Link's X ∈ [Red X − 12, Red X + 4]. Because the Red's X is
    Link's X AND `$F0`, **a Link standing at X ≡ 8 (mod 16) — e.g. on the N/S door column X `$78` — is never hit by the
    vertical shot of a Red that appeared while he stood there** (distance 12). At X ≡ 0 (mod 16) it hits.
  - A **horizontal** shot hits if |Red Y − Link Y| < 9: Link must move a whole 16-px row up or down (8 px is not enough)
    before frame 79.
- **Practical rule:** the Red appears on Link's row or column, facing him, 32-80 px away; Link has 79 frames: either
  kill it (one magic hit, or one beam at full hearts, which travels the same line and hits it during the fade-in), or
  get off the line. A Red that appeared above/below Link standing at X ≡ 8 (mod 16) is harmless as long as he stays
  within that column window.

### 4.3 Blue Wizzrobe: walk, teleport, shooting

- **Walking phase:** `ObjTimer` = `Random,X` OR `$70` (112-127 or 240-255 frames). While the timer ≥ `$10` it steps 1 px
  on even frames (0.5 px/frame); at `$0F`-`$02` it stands 14 frames; at 1 it picks a teleport. (code
  `asm:Wizzrobe_Blue_WalkOrTeleport`, `asm:Wizzrobe_Blue_AlignWithNearestSquareAndRandomizeTimer`)
- **Turning:** every `$40` steps (128 frames) it turns toward Link on one axis, alternating horizontal and vertical;
  each teleport flips which axis is next; a direction change snaps it to a square. (code
  `asm:Wizzrobe_Blue_TurnTowardLink`)
- **Teleport:** to a random diagonal spot `$20` px away on both axes, if that tile is walkable (only the target is
  tested, not the path), else another walking phase. Walking into a block (`$B0`-`$B3`) or water (≥ `$F4`) starts a
  `$20`-px teleport straight through it; any other wall reverses it. A teleport takes 32 frames at 1 px per axis per
  frame; on frames with odd distance left it is neither drawn nor tested (can neither be hit nor hurt). (code
  `asm:Wizzrobe_Blue_ChooseTeleportTarget`, `asm:Wizzrobe_Blue_MoveAndCheckTile`, `asm:Wizzrobe_Blue_BeginTeleporting`)
- **Shooting:** only when not teleporting, on frames with `FrameCounter AND $1F = 0` (once per 32 frames, also during
  the 14-frame pause), and only if it **already faces** Link: row test = same Y high nibble (a 16-px band); column test
  = Link's X **exactly equal** to the Blue's X AND `$F0`. (code `asm:Wizzrobe_Blue_TryShooting`,
  `asm:Wizzrobe_Blue_TryShooting_CheckColumn`)
  - Consequence (code): **a Link at X ≡ 8 (mod 16) is never shot by a Blue vertically**; only rows are dangerous, and
    only in front of a Blue already facing him. Striking from the side or from behind avoids every shot (level-6.md).
- **Kill:** 3 magic hits ≥ 32 frames apart (immunity), no knockback, touch active throughout: stand 10-24 px from it
  on its side (sword reach: the sword point is ~`$10` px ahead, the touch needs < 9 px middle-to-middle). (code,
  inferred)

## 6. Gohma (type `$34`, room 0x1c), from the code

- **Start:** Gohma at (80,70), no spawn cloud; Link at (78,CD) facing up (S key door). (code `asm:Gohma_Init`; trace)
- **Movement:** picks right (80/256), left (80/256) or down (96/256), moves 1 px every 2 frames for `$20` px (64
  frames), reverses, comes back `$20` px, one still frame, picks again: always back at (80,70), within X `$60`-`$A0`, Y
  `$70`-`$90`; never up first. (code `asm:Gohma_Update`)

## 10. Hard problems, ranked

1. **0x38 and 0x3a: forced Wizzrobe + Like-Like + Bubble fights with no refill and no retreat (0x38).** A Red shot is 4
   hearts and unblockable; two volleys plus one Blue hit kill a Link arriving with 9-10 hearts; Reds re-appear every 208
   frames relative to Link; Blues need 3 hits 32 frames apart and teleport; the Bubble takes the sword for 160 frames.
   *Why today's approach may fail:* the observation hides the Red's phase (hidden/fading/solid, frames to the shot) and
   mislabels flashing Wizzrobes as harmless; trajectories/curricula guide movement to exits, not fights; argmax act
   modes are fragile in long fights; the chain hands 0x38 whatever hp is left, and 0x3a what 0x38 left. *What it takes:*
   observation features for Wizzrobe state (Red `ObjState` → phase and frames to shot; Blue timer/teleport; shot lines),
   the harmless-flag fix, training starts with the chain's hp distribution, `room_kills` staging (`ram_randomization`,
   round 24) as a curriculum (1, 2, ... monsters left), rewards for leaving a Red's line before its shot and for killing
   Reds first; probably the planner as a teacher here (Lee's answer 1 in Lee's decisions of Nov 16,
   `docs/routes/rest-of-game.md` "What this is").

   multiply; a 70% 0x38 and a 60% 0x3a already put D6 under 40% before the crossings. *What it takes:* chain-hp-aware
   training, damage penalties in crossing rooms (tried and dropped for D1-D3 crossings, round 6 — but here every heart
   is a later room's survival), and measuring each room from chain states.

   arrow; zero-miss rupee budget at the low end; fireballs every 65 frames; a missed opening means ~450 frames of
