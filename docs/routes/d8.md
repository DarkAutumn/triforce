# Dungeon 8 and the leg from Dungeon 6 (rest-of-game research, Nov 15-16)

This covers the overworld leg from the Dungeon 6 triforce (0x22) by the recorder's whirlwind to Dungeon 8's
entrance tree at 0x6d, then level 8 on the draft path up to its triforce. It was mined from the disassembly, the
level documents and the project's code before any of it is built. `docs/routes/rest-of-game.md` is the synthesis
over this document and its five sibling documents (`d7.md`, `d6.md`, `d9.md`, `late-monsters.md`,
`late-mechanics.md`).

**Marks.** **(trace)** = seen in the running game, as a level document or `game.yaml` records it. **(code)** = read
from the disassembly (`asm:Name`). **(data)** = the ROM tables in `zelda-asm/data/`. **(draft)** = claimed by
`docs/routes/first-quest.md` or `triforce/draft_routes.yaml`. **(inferred)** = reasoning, not checked; check it
before building on it. **(project)** = the project's own code, docs or measurements.

**Sources read.** `zelda-asm/levels/level-8.md` (all of it), `zelda-asm/data/levelblock-uw-7to9-q1.md`,

## 0. Summary

- **The leg** comes down to one number, `asm:TeleportingLevelIndex` (`$523`). Every overworld play of the recorder
  steps it, whether or not a whirlwind comes. RAM `$520`-`$7FE` is zeroed only at reset, so the index survives
  rooms, levels, saving and death (code). On the route the index is `$00` at power-on.
  - At 0x0b (pieces 1-5), two plays facing down leave it at `$FB` (level 4): Link lands on 0x45.
  - Nothing steps it after that: 0x42's pond play is a Q1 flute secret and makes no summon, and dungeon plays never
    step it. So at 0x22 (pieces 1-7) it still reads `$FB`. Two plays facing down make `$FA` (L3, the whirlwind is
    made) and then `$F9` (L2): Link lands on 0x3c at (`$80`, `$AD`).
  - The draft's counts are right for this route (code + trace).
  - The robust rule: **after any ride, `index AND 7` = the level just landed at, minus 1.** Plan each leg from the
    last landing plus every play since it, including plays whose whirlwind was missed.
  - The tree of 0x6d burns from a standing fire in its last 2 frames, within `$10` px of the tree on both axes. Facing
    W, Link must stand at X `$B1`-`$CF` on row Y `$5D`.
  - The red candle has no per-room limit, only the two fire slots. The blue candle allows one fire per room entry.
- **Level 8** on the draft is the level document's short route (`$7E $6E $5E $4E $3E $3F $2F $4C $3C $2C`). It
  drops the book (route steps 1-8), the magic key (steps 13-20) and the bomb on 0x3e's N wall (step 12).
  - Keys: 2 come in, 0x5e's key is taken (+1), 0x4e N is spent (-1), 0x4c's corner key is taken (+1): **3 go out.**
    That matches the draft, but only if the 4c corner detour is walked: 58 extra squares among 8 Pols Voices, about
    900 frames by trace. It also relies on D6's 0x2d fight upstream.
  - Bombs: 1 comes in and 2 walls need bombs, so 1 bomb is cheated at 0x4c. That matches the draft, unless 0x3f is
    fought for its 4 bombs.
- **Blue Darknuts:** HP `$80`, 2 magic-sword hits, 2 bombs. Speed 0.625 px/frame. They are never stunned and
  never shoot. The shield is parried when (weapon dir OR Darknut dir) = `$0C` or `$03`, i.e. an exactly opposite
  hit. A touch costs 2 hearts: 13 hearts survive 6 touches and the 7th kills.
  - 0x5e (5 Darknuts, 4 corner statues) and 0x3e (6, two statues in the middle of the room) are forced fights.
  - "Fought in parts": the partial kills of a visit are banked in `LevelKillCounts` and stay until the level is
    loaded again, **even after the room leaves the 6-room history**. Only a fully cleared room depends on the history.
  - The draft's wording ("kept while it is among the last six rooms") is wrong for partial kills.
- **Manhandlas:** 0x6e is crossed and its N wall bombed with the one bomb Link has. 0x2e is not on the draft path
  (it belongs to the magic-key route).
- **4e, 3f, 4c:** 0x3f is one-way and ends in a corridor one square wide; a Darknut in it facing Link cannot be
  hit (inferred). 0x4c's Pols Voices take 3 magic hits each, die to one arrow (which flies on), and ignore bombs.
- **Gleeok:** one shared HP byte, `$A0` and then `$60` per head: 9 magic hits at 32 frames or more apart. Up to 3
  unkillable flying heads, one 1-heart fireball at a time, and the S wall open for retreat. A retreat resets him to 4
  heads (inferred from the code). Compared with D4's boss-gleeok it is the same hit count (8 white vs 9 magic) but
  three times the flying heads.
- **The hardest problem** is the hearts budget. 13 hearts, no refill, two Blue Darknut rooms with shooting statues,
  three crossings at 2 hearts a touch, then Gleeok. Today's D4 Gleeok already kills 27% of D4 runs, arriving with 2.9
  hearts (project).

## 2. The leg: 0x22 → whirlwind → 0x3c, 0x4c, 0x4d, 0x5d, 0x6d

### 2.1 What the recorder does on the overworld (code)

- Every use plays the tune and freezes all play for `$98` = 152 frames. In Q1 room 0x42 it makes the pond secret
  instead of a summon. Everywhere else in the overworld, in mode 5, it calls `Whirlwind_Summon`. In a dungeon it only
  sets `FluteUsedState`; in a cave (mode `$B`) only the tune plays. (code, `asm:Flute_Wield`)
- `Whirlwind_Summon` (code):
  1. `Whirlwind_StepLevelIndex`: +1 when Link faces right or up (`ObjDir AND $09`), -1 when he faces left or down.
  2. While the level `(index AND 7) + 1` has no piece in `InvTriforce`, step again the same way.
  3. With no piece at all it steps once and returns.
  4. Then, only if `IsWhirlwindSummoned` = 0, no whirlwind is carrying Link and a slot in `$01`-`$0B` is free, it
     makes the whirlwind. **The index has stepped either way.**
- The destination is read **at pickup**, not at the play: `LDA TeleportingLevelIndex / AND #7 /
  Whirlwind_PrevRoomIds`. So a second play before the pickup re-aims the whirlwind already out.
  (code, `asm:Whirlwind_Update_Full@CheckCollision`)
- Landing rooms for L1-L8: 0x37, 0x3c, 0x74, 0x45, 0x0b, 0x22, 0x42, 0x6d. Drop Y: `$8D`, `$AD`, `$8D`, `$8D`, `$AD`,
  `$8D`, `$AD`, `$5D`. Link is released at X `$80`, 86 frames after play starts in the room.
  (code, `asm:Whirlwind_PrevRoomIds`, `asm:Whirlwind_DropOffYs`; trace in items.md)
- Persistence:
  - `$523` is above `$51F`, so a room entry does not clear it. Only `Reset_ClearRam` does, at power-on or reset.
    (code, `Variables.inc` header, `asm:Reset_ClearRam`)
  - `IsWhirlwindSummoned` (`$508`) is cleared on every room entry: one whirlwind per room visit. (code)
- Pickup:
  - It uses `Link_CheckMonsterTouch`, which reports nothing while Link is invincible, stunned, halted or holding the
    clock. A missed whirlwind leaves at X ≥ `$F0` and the room allows no second one. (code)
  - The pickup runs the harm path with 0 damage: it sets Link's invincibility to `$18` and zeroes `WorldKillCount`,
    `HelpDropCount` and `IsHelpDropBomb`. (code)

**Why the destination depends on every earlier play, and the rule that removes the dependence** (code + inferred):

- After a ride, the index points at the level just landed at. Every later overworld play shifts it by one owned level.
  That includes plays whose whirlwind never came: a missed one, a second one in the same room, or no free slot.
- So the plays needed at a recorder step are a function of `($523 AND 7, InvTriforce, target level)` alone. Read them
  from RAM; do not count them from the route.
- One extra play anywhere breaks the next leg. Examples:
  - A model's stray RECORDER on the overworld.
  - The 0x0b second play coming too late: Link lands on 0x0b itself (trace in items.md: 477 frames). That leaves
    `$FC`, and one play facing down then reaches 0x45.
- After all 8 pieces, every play steps exactly one level.

**The second play's window** (inferred from the code):

- It must come after the first freeze ends (152 frames) and before the pickup.
- The whirlwind spawns at X = 0 on Link's Y and plays its spawn cloud: about 13-25 frames, depending on its slot.
  Then it moves 2 px a frame. Pickup comes when the midpoints are less than 9 px apart: from X `$70`, about 52
  updates.
- So the window is roughly 60-80 frames. The 0x0b trace pressed on the first free frame after the freeze.
- After the second play the freeze stops the whirlwind too.

**Failure modes at 0x22** (code + inferred):

- **0x22's red Lynel.** Its sword shot costs 2 hearts and only the magic shield parries it. A hit makes Link
  invincible for about 48 frames, the whirlwind passes through him, and the room will not make another.
  - Recovery: leave the room (S into 0x32) and come back.
  - From `$F9` the net step must be 0: one play facing down, then one facing up, gives L1 and then L2. Two plays
    facing down from `$F9` would reach L8, which is not yet held, and skip on to L7 = 0x42. **Wrong.**
- **Moving before playing.** Link comes out of a level facing down (code: `asm:Room_EnterCommon`, mechanics
  "Levels"). Any step changes his facing; a right or up facing steps the index +1.

### 2.4 Burning the tree (code)

- The candle fire appears `$10` px ahead of Link, moves `$10` more at 0.5 px a frame (31-32 frames), then stands for
  63 frames and frees its slot. Tiles and room bounds do not stop it. (`asm:Candle_Wield`, `asm:Fire_Update`)
- `Tree_Update` burns the tree only in Q1 for 0x6d. It looks at slot `$10` first and slot `$11` only if `$10` holds no
  standing fire. The standing fire's `ObjTimer` must be < 2 (its last 2 frames) and its midpoint less than `$10` px
  from the tree on both axes. Then it writes the stairs tile `$70` and sets room flag `$80`, which is saved: the
  stairs stay for good.
- **The spot, facing W:** the fire rests at Link X - `$20` and the tree is at X `$A0`. So Link X must be `$B1`-`$CF`
  on row Y `$5D` (fire Y = Link Y is inferred).
  - The draft's "X `$B8`-`$C0`" lies inside this window (draft).
  - The W-side spot is X `$71`-`$8F` facing E (inferred, same arithmetic).
- Stairs appear about 94 frames after the use (items.md); 92 by the Nov 14 walk (`game.yaml` 0x5d note).
- **Per-screen limit:**
  - Blue candle: one fire per room entry (`HasUsedCandle`, cleared on room entry).
  - Red candle: no flag test; the only limit is the two slots `$10`/`$11`, shared with bombs.
  - With two red fires out, the tree tests only slot `$10` while it holds a standing fire, so the fire in `$10` must
    be the one on the tree (inferred from `Tree_Update`'s order).
- The fire hurts Link for ½ heart if he walks into it (`asm:Fire_Update@DrawAndCheckCollisions`). A harm also zeroes
  the help-drop counters.
- Leaving the room before the fire burns out wastes it (room entry clears the weapon slots; code, mechanics
  "Mode 6 init"). The red candle is free to use again.
- The trigger is the end of the fire, not its start. A model must use CANDLE W at the spot and then **wait** about 94
  frames without walking W into its own fire.

### 4.3 "Fought in parts through the entry door": the exact rule (code, `asm:RoomHistory_SaveKillCountUW`, `asm:RoomHistory_ModifyObjCountUW`)

- **When Link leaves a dungeon room** (mode 6 init, also by stairs):
  - If `RoomKillCount` ≥ `RoomObjCount` (all made this visit are dead), the room is marked **all dead**:
    `LevelKillCounts[room]` = `$0F` and flags bits 6-7 = 3.
  - Otherwise `LevelKillCounts[room]` += this visit's kills, and flags bits 6-7 = min(sum, 2).
  - Bubbles never count: they cannot die and are not in `RoomKillCount`.
- **When Link enters**:
  - If the room is in the 6-entry history, **or its flags are not "all dead"**, it makes `count -
    LevelKillCounts[room]` monsters (none if negative).
  - If it is all dead and out of the history, ordinary monsters are forgotten (flags and the count cleared) and the
    full set comes back. The non-recurring ones stay dead (list id `$32`-`$48` except `$3A`/`$3B`: Manhandla, Gohma,
    Gleeok and others).
- So:
  - **Partial kills are kept until the level is loaded again** (`asm:Mode2Load_Init` zeroes all 128 counts),
    **whether or not the room is still in the history.**
  - A fully cleared room stays empty only while it is among the last six additions; after that it refills **in full**.
- **When the banked kills are lost:**
  - A level reload: Link walks out of 0x7e S to the overworld and comes back in.
  - For a fully cleared room: 6 more history additions (rooms already in the ring are not re-added, so it is
    additions, not rooms walked; the trace in level-8.md §At a glance).
  - A death and continue clears the history but not `LevelKillCounts` (code, `asm:Mode8Continue_Init` calls only
    `RoomHistory_Clear`). The project ends a run at death anyway.
- **Disagreement:** `docs/routes/first-quest.md` "Banked kills: a room's kills are kept while it is among the last six
  rooms added to the room history". For partial kills that is wrong; the code keeps them past the history.
  level-8.md's trace and wording ("until the level is loaded again") agree with the code.
- **Trace** (level-8.md): in 0x5e, 2 of 5 killed, out by the bombed S wall and back in: 3 Darknuts. 2 more killed, out
  and back: 1. The door Link came by stays open (a bombed or unlocked door is saved for both rooms), so it is always a
  way out of the fight.
- **What a re-entry gives** (code; inferred where noted):
  - The shutters are closed again and the statues' timers are 0.
  - The remaining Darknuts are placed fresh, with spawn clouds, at spawn spots not within `$22` px of Link on both
    axes (`asm:Spawn_IsFarFromLink`).
  - A retreat is therefore also a reset of position and threat (inferred).
- **What the shutters do** (code):
  - They close on every entry, and a shutter Link enters through closes behind him.
  - They open only while the trigger "no monsters left" holds (Bubbles and fireballs don't count), one at a time, 8
    frames each, in the order N, S, W, E.
  - The type is read from the room being left. So 0x4e S (open on 0x4e's side) always leads back into 0x5e, and 0x5e
    then opens at once if it is still remembered as empty.
- **Chain-runner limits** (project, `crates/env/src/chain.rs`):
  - Without a route `retreat`, a back-and-forth trips `MAX_BOUNCES` = 5 returns in a row.
  - With one, `MAX_RETREATS` = 3 retreats per **run**: one cap for both rooms and every other retreat on the route.

## 10. Hard problems, ranked

1. **The hearts budget through Dungeon 8 with no refill.**
   - Why it is hard: 13 hearts must pay for 0x6e, two forced Blue Darknut rooms with shooting statues, two crossings
     of mixed rooms at 2 hearts a touch (0x4e, then the one-way 0x3f), 0x4c's Pols Voices (plus the 58-square key
     detour), the tunnel, then a 9-hit Gleeok with 3 flying heads.
   - Today's evidence: Link reaches D4's Gleeok with 2.9 hearts and that Gleeok kills 27% of D4 runs. The Olive Garden
     costs 3.3 hearts a visit.
   - Why per-room models may fail: each is trained from refilled entry states (`write_entry_state`) and is judged on
     its own room's success, then hearts. Nothing makes 0x5e's model save hearts for Gleeok five rooms later.
     Harvested states fix the distribution, not the incentive.
   - What it would take: hearts-at-exit in the objective (backlog: "leave rooms with more hearts"), heart-drop
     pickup in the fight rooms (about 5 hearts expected from 11 blue kills, §4.1), Gleeok trained from harvested
     low-heart arrivals, and possibly the planner (Lee's decisions of Nov 16, `docs/routes/rest-of-game.md` "What
     this is", answer 1).
2. **Two Blue Darknut rooms with statues, back to back.**
   - The darknut family has never seen statue fire (the Olive Garden and 0x64 have none). Its D5 numbers rely on
     bombs (73% at 0 bombs) and the white sword. In D8, on a run with no drops, it has 0 bombs and the magic sword.
   - Both rooms must be won: about 0.8 × 0.8 if independent (inferred).
   - The statue fireballs are aimed at Link's position, so a model that stands still to wait for a Darknut's flank is
     punished.
   - What it would take: D8 states and curricula with staged kills (`room_kills`, as for the Garden). Possibly train
     the retreat (fight in parts), which the code supports. A retreat cap that allows it.
3. **Gleeok-4 at low hearts.**
   - Up to 3 flying heads that chase and cannot die; a fireball always in flight; attacks only from beneath the heads'
     sweep, or beams at full hearts.
   - D4's model learned 1 flying head and 8 white hits from the W door.
   - A retreat resets the fight (inferred).
   - What it would take: boss-gleeok trained on 8:0x3c (entry S) at the hearts the chain delivers. Reward the bottom
     rows' safety implicitly through hearts kept.
4. **0x3f: one-way, with a 1-wide corridor before the stairs.**
   - A Darknut facing W in the corridor is unhittable from Link's side. The W shutter forbids retreat. 2 Bubbles can
     take the sword.
   - What it would take: the crossing must learn to wait for the corridor to clear, or to fight the room (also +4
     bombs, which needs Lee's word).
5. **Whirlwind exactness.**
   - Wrong play counts, a stray play, a hit before pickup (0x22's Lynel) or a moved facing all send Link to the wrong
     dungeon. The index lives in RAM, not in the observation.
   - A model would need `$523`, the pieces and its facing, and would have to learn a counting task with no gradient.

7. **0x4c with the corner key:** 73 squares among 8 random hoppers that cross blocks. The pols-voice family exists
