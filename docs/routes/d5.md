# Dungeon 5 (`d2first` steps 186-208, round 24)

`triforce/routes.yaml` `d2first` ends with the draft's 23 Dungeon 5 steps (`triforce/draft_routes.yaml`
`first-quest` until round 24) with the owner's notes of Nov 14 as rules, after the leg from the D4 triforce
(`docs/routes/d5-leg.md`, steps 134-185; the mountain stretch rejoins on 5:0x76, step 186). Route `d5` (`extends:
d2first`, the slice's scouting route) is gone. Until states from the played entrance exist the dungeon is played
from development states (`triforce.probe.write_entry_state(path, 5, dict(D5_INVENTORY, bombs=N), states_dir)`:
written inventory, never installed). Tests: `tests/test_d5.py` (the running game), `crates/zelda/tests/objectives.rs`
`d5_*`, `crates/zelda/src/objectives.rs` `d5_takes_the_recorder_in_the_olive_gardens_cellar`.
