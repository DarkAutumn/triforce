# Dungeon 5 (route `d5`, round 24)

`triforce/routes.yaml` `d5` is `{extends: d2first, steps: [...]}`: d2first, then the draft's 23 Dungeon 5 steps
(`triforce/draft_routes.yaml` `first-quest`) with the owner's notes of Nov 14 as rules. The leg from the D4 triforce
to Dungeon 5 (another slice) joins it later; until then the dungeon is played from a development state at 5:0x76
(`triforce.probe.write_entry_state(path, 5, dict(D5_INVENTORY, bombs=N), states_dir)`: written inventory, never
installed). Tests: `tests/test_d5.py` (the running game), `crates/zelda/tests/objectives.rs` `d5_*`,
`crates/zelda/src/objectives.rs` `d5_takes_the_recorder_in_the_olive_gardens_cellar`.

## Ledgers

- **Keys:** 6 in; spent at 0x06 W, 0x27 W, 0x25 W; taken in 0x66 (way back) and 0x27: 5 out.
- **Bombs:** a free family bombs only above the step's reserve: 8 everywhere (Link holds at most 8) but the Olive
  Garden's first visit and Digdogger (0). The walls: one bomb each (0x66 W, 0x65 W; gibdo `required` and round
  22-23's wall mask). The 10th damage-free kill's bomb stays allowed in every room (every mode but `never`). The
  four bombs of 0x65 come back on the way east.
- **Hearts:** 10 containers in, 11 after Digdogger.
- **Forced fights:** 0x05, 0x66 on the way back, Digdogger, and 0x64 with clips off.
