### 1.1 The whirlwind count (0x6d → 0x37)

- What decides the destination (code, `Whirlwind_Summon`, `Whirlwind_StepLevelIndex`): each recorder play **on the
  overworld in mode 5** steps `TeleportingLevelIndex` (`$523`) by +1 when Link faces right or up, -1 when he faces
  left or down, then keeps stepping the same way past levels whose piece is missing. Destination level =
  (`$523` AND 7) + 1; the screen scrolls in from `Whirlwind_PrevRoomIds` = `$36,$3B,$73,$44,$0A,$21,$41,$6C`, so
  level 1 → room 0x37. Plays in a dungeon (they only set `FluteUsedState`) and the Q1 pond play in 0x42 (it reveals
  the stairs, `Flute_Wield@RevealSecret`) do not step the index (code, `Flute_Wield`).
- Count at 0x6d (inferred from the draft's plays): the last overworld ride was 0x22 → 0x3c (level 2), so the index
  reads level 2 (`$523` AND 7 = 1). After Dungeon 8 Link holds all eight pieces, so no level is skipped: **one play
  facing down** → index 0 → level 1 → 0x37. Link comes out of a dungeon facing down, so he plays at once (draft:
  "the recorder once, facing down: Dungeon 1's entrance"). Facing up or right would give level 3 (0x74).
- Any extra overworld play between 0x22 and 0x6d (none on the draft: 0x3c, 0x4c, 0x4d, 0x5d, then 0x6d's tree burned
  with the candle) shifts this count (draft "What the game does": "an extra one anywhere changes the later counts").
- Pickup (code, `Whirlwind_Update_Full@CheckCollision`): the whirlwind is made at X 0 on Link's Y, moves right 2
  px/frame, picks Link up through `Link_CheckMonsterTouch` (midpoints < 9 px). **It misses while Link is invincible,
  stunned, halted, held or holding the clock**; 0x6d has 4 red Moblins (`$04`, edge spawn; arrows 0.5 heart, any
  shield parries) (data). A missed whirlwind leaves at the right edge after 120 frames; a second play in the same
  room makes no whirlwind (`IsWhirlwindSummoned`) **but steps the index anyway**.
- Recovery from a missed pickup (inferred from the code and the draft's observation that a second play before the
  whirlwind arrives re-steps the destination): the index then already reads level 1. Leave and re-enter 0x6d
  (`IsWhirlwindSummoned` lives in the RAM zeroed on room entry), play facing down (→ level 8, whirlwind made), then
  facing up before it reaches Link (→ level 1). Not walked.
- Arrival (code, `Whirlwind_InitDropOffAndBeginUpdate`, `Whirlwind_DropOffYs`): 0x37, Link halted at X 0, Y `$8D`,
  rides 64 frames after the whirlwind's spawn cloud and is released at X `$80`; (trace, per the code comment)
  released 86 frames after play starts in the room. The pickup also zeroes `WorldKillCount`/`HelpDropCount` (code).
- One recorder play freezes the world `$98` = 152 frames (code, `Flute_Wield`).

## 2. Level 9: rules that hold in every room

- Start: room 0x76 at (`$78`,`$DD`) facing up; first movable at Y `$CD` (trace). The S door of 0x76 leads back to
  overworld 0x05 at (`$50`,`$9D`) (trace, 201 frames of Down from the door square).
- Walk-in on every door entry (code, mechanics.md: `Room_EnterCommon@Method2`, `@ChooseWalkDistance`): Link is put at
  the room's edge (X `$00`/`$F0`, Y `$3D`/`$DD`) and walks in before he can be controlled. **First-movable spots
  (walked, every arrival of the draft's way; the `$18`/`$28` px this document inferred were 8 px too far):** through
  an open doorway or a key door `$10` px in: from S Y `$CD`, from N Y `$4D`, from W X `$10`, from E X `$E0`; through
  a bombed wall or a shutter `$20` px in: Y `$BD`, Y `$5D`, X `$20`, X `$D0`. **The first-movable spots in §3.1's
  table follow the old arithmetic: read them 8 px nearer the door** (0x30's arrival is (`$D0`,`$8D`), the right-hand
  Traps' own column). Tunnel and cellar arrivals are fixed spots (table 3.2).
- Persistence (code + trace, level-9.md "At a glance"): opened key doors, bombed walls and taken room items stay;
  **shutters close again and every push block is back on each room entry**. Kill counts: a room stays empty only
  while it is among the last six room ids entered (cellars count); dead Patras and Ganon never return; the
  Lanmolas of 0x55 are never saved as all dead (`RoomObjCount` 8 vs at most 2 room kills), **but a head's death
  is banked against a list count of 1, so once one head has died the room is empty on every later entry**
  (walked, `d9-fights-walks.md` §1; this document said they are back on every entry).
- Dark rooms (0x65, 0x15, 0x41, 0x42 on the route): only the palette is dark; nothing else reads the flag (code,
  `Room_IsDark_Bank5`). A pixel observation would be blind there; a RAM observation is not affected (inferred).
- Sand (0x11, 0x21, 0x31, 0x46): no slowdown in dungeons; the slow tiles are an overworld rule (code, mechanics.md,
  `Link_InitSpeed`).
- Death: CONTINUE puts Link in 0x76 with 3 hearts, keeps doors, walls, items, the silver arrow and dead Patras,
  clears the room history (cleared rooms refill, blocks back, Lanmolas back) (code, `Mode8Continue_*`).
- Wallmasters, false walls, hungry Goriya, heart containers, Triforce pieces: none in this level (data).
- Statues: only layout `$23` (0x37, 0x53, off the route) shoots; the statues of 0x76, 0x32, 0x42 do nothing (code,
  `Statue_RoomLayoutIds`).
- Hitting a dungeon person (0x06's man) turns on fireballs from (`$48`,`$82`) and (`$A8`,`$86`) until Link leaves
  (code, `Person_CheckCollisions`): mask A against him.

### 13.2 What the engine learned for it

`docs/architecture.md` "The leg to Dungeon 9, Dungeon 9 and the end" says where each piece lives. In short:

- **The end of the game**: a route step's `exit: end` (the route's last step, in a dungeon room, on no stretch) is
  reached on the first frame of game mode `$13` (asm:Zelda_State1WaitForEnding); the objective's target in that
  room is the row under Zelda (asm:Zelda_Update); the single-room ending is `game-completed`, a chain run ends
  "the game ended in ...". A recorded run that ends so is a `win` replay with that reason and verifies (20 of 20
  runs from Zelda's door, §13.3).
- **Waypoints `ring` and `silver-arrow`**, and both in what a step is matched by: the two passes of tunnel 0x75,
  and every room after cellar 0x00, are told apart by what Link holds.
- **Kept kills** (`banked_kills` on a step): a start in a room the route comes back to banks that many kills
  after the fresh-room write, so the room makes what the game makes there (0x07 and 0x10 their Bubbles, 0x20 and
  0x61 nothing). Values read from the running game: 5, 5, 15, 15.
- **`sword: false`** on a step (the old man of 9:0x06: striking him brings fireballs for the rest of the visit):
  SWORD and BEAMS masked on that step.
- **Doors**: `never_opens` on 0x41 S and 0x31 S (the path search does not go through them); `keep_out` on 0x63 N
  and S and on 0x26 S and W; `requires_pieces: 8` on 0x66.
- **The burned tree from above** (0:0x47): `burn_facings` knows S from the tiles of the tree's column above it.
- **A rock wall over a level's stairs** (0:0x05) as a bomb target: before, BOMB was never offered under it (the
  level entrance read as "south of the room"), which is why no model placed a bomb there on the walk.
- **0:0x17** has a path cost on the row under its top water (pressing Up there lays the ladder northward).
- **Patra**: the children are the fight's targets before the body. **Lanmola**: nothing new (a segment's dead
  dummy was already a kill). **Ganon**: targets by phase (hidden: his position + 8, the square a stab reaches him
  from; shown: both jump spots; brown: the tiles of his row and column two squares clear of him; dying: his ashes,
  then the Triforce of Power as the room's treasure); ARROW offered only while he is brown, facing him, from his
  row or column; `requires_rupees: 1` in his room while he lives.
- **Families**: lanmola (9:0x55), patra (0x16, 0x27, 0x61, 0x52), ganon (0x42, 0x32), all `trainable: false` until
  they have start states; wizzrobe +ten rooms of level 9 (fourteen steps); like-like +0x14, 0x51, 0x41, 0x03;
  equipment-room +the seven cellars; ow-zora `candle: true`; ow-lynel 0x05's wall.
