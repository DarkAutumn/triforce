# Dungeon 7 and the leg from Dungeon 5 (rest-of-game research, Nov 15-16)

The stretch from the Dungeon 5 triforce (overworld 0x0b) through the whirlwind, the two shops (arrows, food) and
0x42's pond to Dungeon 7 and its triforce, mined from the disassembly, the level documents and the project's code
before any of it is built. §0 proposes the route steps 209-247; §9 is the inventory of what exists and what is
missing for exactly this stretch; §10 is the work list. `docs/routes/rest-of-game.md` is the synthesis over this
document and its five siblings (`d6.md`, `d8.md`, `d9.md`, `late-monsters.md`, `late-mechanics.md`).

Marks: **(trace)** walked in the game per a level doc / round doc; **(code)** read in `crates/` or `triforce/` or the
disassembly's code citations; **(data)** game.yaml / ROM level data / generated doc tables; **(draft)**
`triforce/draft_routes.yaml` notes; **(inferred)** reasoning, not checked. Unless named, "doc" =
`zelda-asm/levels/level-7.md`. Numbers: white sword `$20` per hit; Link has 11 containers from Digdogger to
Aquamentus, 12 after (data: `routes.yaml`, entry_states HEARTS rule). Small shield only (no magic shield on the
route).

## 1. The leg, room by room

### 0:0x0b, out of Dungeon 5 → the whirlwind
- Enter: the D5 triforce sends Link out at 0x0b's exit spot facing down (doc pattern for 7; `mechanics.md` "Mode $A": exit
  spot, facing down) (code). 1 Red Leever (half a heart) (data).
- Must: RECORDER twice facing down, the second on the first free frame after the 152-frame freeze, before the
  whirlwind arrives; then be touched by it (trace, items.md "On the overworld, the whirlwind": index 0, pieces 1-5: one
  play lands in 0x0b itself, two in 0x45, 625 frames).
- Mechanism (code): each overworld play that reaches the summon steps `TeleportingLevelIndex` ($523) +1 facing right/up,
  −1 facing left/down, skipping levels whose piece is missing; plays in a dungeon or in 0x42 don't step it; the index
  persists between rooms. One whirlwind per room entry; created at X 0 on Link's Y, moving right 2 px/frame; pickup is a
  monster touch, refused while Link is invincible/stunned/halted.
- Goes wrong (code/inferred): wrong facing on a play or a third play → another dungeon's landing (or 0x0b itself); a hit
  by the Leever before pickup → whirlwind passes → none again until the room is re-entered, and the index has already
  stepped, so the next try counts from the new value. Link moving off the row → miss.
- Perceive: facing, plays made (not in the observation), the whirlwind object ($2E, observed as an `Other` entity: code
  `observation.rs` gives every nonzero ObjType a slot). The index is RAM only.
- Code today: RECORDER masked on the overworld (`actions::recorder_wanted`: dungeon + big Digdogger only) (code); no
  `exit: recorder`/`entry: recorder` in routes.yaml (`STEP_EXITS`, `STEP_ENTRIES` in `game_map.rs:263-265`) (code);
  `route_entry` returns None for a non-neighbour jump (`objectives.rs:242-244`) (code).
