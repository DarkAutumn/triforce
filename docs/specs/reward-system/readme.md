# Reward System Specs — Status Overview

These spec documents were generated by an AI agent that audited the reward system and
proposed improvements. This readme tracks what has been done, what remains to try, and
what has become obsolete because the code took a different direction.

---

## Done

These proposals have been implemented (sometimes with a different mechanism than originally
proposed, but the underlying problem is addressed).

### PBRS for movement (movement-rewards, reward-philosophy)
The old ad-hoc movement rewards (closer/away/lateral/off-wavefront/wall-collision/danger)
were **all removed** and replaced with proper Potential-Based Reward Shaping:
`F(s,s') = Φ(s') − Φ(s)` where `Φ(s) = −wavefront_distance / PBRS_SCALE`. γ=1 so round
trips cancel exactly.  Weapon/item actions only get PBRS when they cause a tile change,
preventing ratchet exploits.

### `remove_rewards()` replaced with `scale_rewards()` (reward-philosophy, health-rewards, final-review)
The #1 structural problem across 6 specs.  Instead of stripping all positive rewards on
damage, the code now scales them by 0.5×.  Equipment pickups (`reward-gained-*`) are
exempt, so picking up a sword while taking a hit still registers.

### Health penalty scaled by damage (health-rewards)
`penalty = −REWARD_SMALL × half_hearts`, capped at REWARD_MAXIMUM.  A Gel touch
(0.5 hearts) now costs less than a Red Lynel hit (2.0 hearts).

### Health gain scaled by urgency (health-rewards)
`reward = REWARD_SMALL + REWARD_MEDIUM × (1 − health / max_health)`.  Healing at low HP
is now worth more than healing at high HP.

### Beam reward higher than melee (combat-rewards)
Beam hit = REWARD_MEDIUM (0.5), melee = REWARD_SMALL (0.25).  Added a beam-distance
bonus (REWARD_TINY) for hits from >48 pixels away.

### Bomb economics fixed (combat-rewards, equipment-rewards)
Hit reward increased to REWARD_MEDIUM (0.5), use penalty decreased to REWARD_SMALL (0.25).
A single-enemy bomb hit is now net positive (+0.25), not net negative.

### Key usage re-enabled (equipment-rewards, final-review)
`critique_used_key()` is active and awards REWARD_SMALL (0.25).

### Entity observation expanded (observation-space)
8 features per entity slot (was 4): presence, dir_x, dir_y, health, stun, hurts_on_touch,
killable, secret.  Plus a separate `entity_types` tensor with 40+ unified type IDs.

### Wallmaster bug fixed (combat-rewards, special-cases)
The `return` that exited the entire `critique_attack` method on encountering a close
wallmaster was changed to `continue`.

### Ad-hoc movement penalties removed (movement-rewards, reward-philosophy)
Wall collision (−0.25), lateral move (−0.01), off-wavefront, stuck-tile, and binary danger
(−0.50) penalties are all gone.  PBRS handles navigation; room stalling provides urgency.

### Room stalling penalty redesigned (special-cases, movement-rewards)
Replaced the unbounded stuck-tile penalty with a bounded ramp: 150-step grace period, then
−0.01 per step ramping to −0.02 over 1850 steps.  Resets on room change, enemy kills,
or item pickups.

### Combat decay system (combat-rewards)
After 8 combat events per room, additional events are scaled by 0.5^(excess).  Prevents
farming respawning enemies for infinite rewards.

### Wrong location penalty reduced (exploration-rewards)
Changed from −REWARD_MAXIMUM (−1.0) to −REWARD_SMALL (−0.25).

### Dead combat constants removed (combat-rewards, movement-rewards)
`FIRED_CORRECTLY_REWARD`, `DIDNT_FIRE_PENALTY`, `INJURE_KILL_MOVEMENT_ROOM_REWARD`,
`MOVEMENT_SCALE_FACTOR`, and `DISTANCE_THRESHOLD` have all been cleaned up.

### IMPALA model architectures (observation-space, cross-attention)
ImpalaSharedAgent and ImpalaMultiHeadAgent with ResNet backbone, CoordConv, spatial
attention pooling, and entity-spatial cross-attention.

### Multi-head action decomposition (movement-actions)
MultiHeadAgent and ImpalaMultiHeadAgent decompose actions into [type, direction] with
per-head policy and joint log-probability.

### Boomerang stun reward (combat-rewards)
REWARD_SMALL with combat decay, tracked per-enemy per-room to prevent farming.

## Not Yet Tried

These proposals are still relevant to the current codebase and could be implemented.

### Wavefront info in observations (observation-space, final-review) — Priority 1
Wavefront is computed every step but never exposed to the agent.  The observation only
includes 4 cardinal direction bits for the next room.  Proposed: add wavefront gradient
direction (4 floats) and normalized distance (1 float) to the info vector.

### Continuous health fraction (observation-space) — Priority 2
Health is still two binary flags: critical (≤1 heart) and full.  The entire middle range
is invisible.  Proposed: replace with `health / max_health` continuous feature.

### Kill bonus separate from hit (combat-rewards, final-review) — Priority 2
All hits give the same reward regardless of whether the enemy dies.  Proposed: add a
separate kill bonus on top of the hit reward to incentivize finishing enemies.

### Tiered equipment rewards (equipment-rewards) — Priority 3
22 of 23 items still award REWARD_MAXIMUM.  Sword = compass = raft = boomerang.
Proposed tiers: critical (sword, triforce) at 1.0, high (bow, boomerang, keys) at 0.75,
moderate (bombs, compass, map) at 0.50.

### Enemy-aware wavefront (wavefront-alternatives) — Priority 3
Wavefront uses uniform BFS costs (Dijkstra with cost=1 per tile).  No enemy proximity
weighting.  During FIGHT objectives, wavefront points toward enemies while the agent
should ideally approach cautiously.  Proposed: Dijkstra with cost
`1 + danger_weight × max(0, radius − manhattan_dist) / radius`.

### Objective transition smoothing (wavefront-alternatives) — Priority 3
When objective kind flips (e.g. FIGHT→MOVE after last enemy dies), the wavefront can
reverse 180° in one step, causing a reward discontinuity.  Proposed: 2–3 step blend of
old/new wavefront during transitions.

### Blocking reward reduction (special-cases) — Priority 3
Still REWARD_MEDIUM (0.5), which is 2× a kill reward.  Creates incentive to stand in
projectile paths.  Proposed: reduce to REWARD_TINY (0.05) or remove entirely since
blocking is passive (shield faces Link's direction automatically).

### Cave attack action masking (special-cases, final-review) — Priority 4
Still uses a −1.0 penalty instead of action masking.  The action masking infrastructure
already exists.  Proposed: mask attack actions in caves via `get_action_mask()`.

### Multi-tile movement (movement-actions) — Priority 4
Movement is still tile-by-tile, and `WS_ADJUSTMENT_FRAMES` is still present.  ~50% of
PPO buffer is MOVE decisions.  Proposed: continue holding direction until interrupted by
wall/enemy/door/max-tiles, compressing N tiles into one buffer entry.

### Inter-room PBRS (exploration-rewards) — Priority 4
PBRS only shapes within a room.  Between rooms, only the flat new/revisit location
rewards apply.  Proposed: `Φ(room) = −A*_distance(current, target)` for room-level
shaping.

### Count-based exploration rewards (exploration-rewards) — Priority 5
Location rewards are binary new/revisit.  Proposed:
`reward = REWARD_LARGE / sqrt(visit_count)` for diminishing returns on revisits.

### Door states in observations (observation-space) — Priority 5
No door open/locked/barred info in the observation vector.  Proposed: 4 features
(N/S/E/W) encoding door state.

### Running return normalization (reward-scaling) — Priority 5
No running mean/std normalization on returns.  Only per-minibatch advantage normalization
exists.  Proposed: `(reward − running_mean) / (running_std + ε)`.

### Wider or soft reward clamping (reward-scaling) — Priority 5
Still hard-clamped to [−1.0, 1.0].  Proposed: widen to [−2, 2] or use soft tanh clipping
to reduce information loss.

### Location tracking bug (exploration-rewards)
`critique_location_change` seeds `_correct_locations` with a `(prev, curr)` tuple on the
first room transition, but later checks for a single `MapLocation`.  The tuple never
matches, making the first-move defense dead code.

### Simplified magnitude scale (reward-scaling) — Priority 5
Still 6 tiers (MINIMUM through MAXIMUM) with non-uniform spacing (5× between some, 1.5×
between others).  Proposed: 3 tiers (SMALL 0.1, MEDIUM 0.5, LARGE 1.0).

### Multi-head value decomposition (reward-scaling, reward-philosophy) — Priority 5
Multi-head policy exists, but value function is still a single scalar.  Proposed: separate
value heads for navigation, combat, survival, and progress.

### Local walkability grid (observation-space) — Priority 5
No 5×5 binary walkability grid in observations.  Would help the agent avoid walls without
trial-and-error.

## Obsolete

These proposals addressed real problems, but the code took a fundamentally different
direction that makes the specific recommendations no longer applicable.

### Graduate danger penalty (movement-rewards)
Proposed scaling the binary −0.50 danger penalty by overlap count.  The danger penalty was
**removed entirely** when PBRS replaced all ad-hoc movement rewards.

### Cap stuck-tile penalty (movement-rewards, special-cases)
Proposed capping the unbounded quadratic stuck-tile penalty at −REWARD_SMALL.  The
stuck-tile penalty was **replaced** with the bounded room-stalling penalty (grace period
+ linear ramp), which is a cleaner design.

### Reduce wall collision to −0.01 (movement-rewards)
Proposed reducing the −0.25 wall collision penalty.  The penalty was **removed entirely**
under PBRS.  Wall collisions are now just wasted steps (zero PBRS delta).

### Credit off-wavefront recovery (movement-rewards)
Proposed rewarding escape from off-wavefront tiles.  Under PBRS, returning to a lower-
distance tile from a higher one naturally produces positive reward.

### Directional dot-product fallback (wavefront-alternatives)
Proposed a fallback for tiles not on the wavefront.  PBRS returns no reward for
unreachable tiles (None check and early return).

### Remove lateral penalty (movement-rewards)
Proposed removing the −0.01 lateral movement penalty.  Removed along with all other
ad-hoc movement penalties when PBRS was adopted.

### Replace `remove_rewards()` with net-damage-trade calculation (health-rewards)
Proposed keeping both hit and damage signals and computing a net trade.  The actual
solution (`scale_rewards(0.5)` with equipment exemption) is simpler and addresses the
same problem without the bookkeeping.

### Fix OverworldSwordCritic post-sword transitions (exploration-rewards)
Mentioned unconditional +0.75 for all room transitions after sword.  The
`OverworldSwordCritic` still exists but the overall location tracking was reworked with
the wrong-location penalty reduction and objective-based routing, so the original concern
about un-gated rewards is less severe.
